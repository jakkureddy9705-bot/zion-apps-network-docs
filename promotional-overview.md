## Positioning

**Start with the business question. Find a useful planning resource. Agree the evidence before implementation.**

Zion Tech Group's reviewed public network brings together app topics, services, browser planning tools, and guides. Use the directory to find a starting point for a service brief, cloud-cost scenario, support workflow, or AI-app evaluation. Then clarify the process, assumptions, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria with the appropriate team.

A catalog page is not proof of a deployed application. The [complete reviewed index](app-index.md) visibly distinguishes retrieved pages, redirects, minimal/stub pages, missing destinations, and unresolved routes. The [interlink map](app-interlinks.md) describes reading relationships, not working integrations.

## Discovery copy and offer controls

A good Discovery starts with one process and ends with a defensible next step: proceed, measure first, wait, or do not automate. The reviewed [Discovery page](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/) is Portuguese and describes a free questionnaire and summary alongside optional separately scoped paid work. Other observed pages contain paid Discovery references. Reconcile the current offer before publishing a universal free-engagement claim or quoting a price.

For this documented rollout, questionnaire access and the client-safe summary are free. A complimentary consulting trial, if offered, needs written scope and duration. Implementation is not included by default, and there is no automatic paid conversion. Do not copy checkout links or advertise an unverified delivery deadline from this documentation.

Email-copy language must state that delivery depends on the provider and recipient inbox. Requested copies should be independently tracked for the client and commercial@ziontechgroup.com using the [routing specification](commercial-routing.md).

## Claims and editorial review table

| Prefer | Avoid unless independently evidenced |
| --- | --- |
| A reviewed directory route or planning resource | A production-ready deployed app |
| A modeled scenario with stated inputs | Guaranteed ROI, savings, payback, or revenue |
| A related reading path | A working integration or installed automation |
| A requested email copy with separately tracked status | Instant or guaranteed inbox delivery |
| A scoped pilot subject to approval | A reserved technician, confirmed availability, or committed delivery date |
| An offer confirmed with Commercial | A universal price or a free/paid assumption from conflicting pages |
| Security/privacy questions for review | Certification or compliance based only on a product name or badge |
