## What a link means

A link here is a documented reading or planning relationship. It does not install a connector, share data, configure credentials, connect infrastructure, run an AI model, reserve a technician, authorize work, or establish compatibility. Resolve those questions in a separately scoped implementation.

The reviewed [network page](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps-network/) displays 58 cards, 189 internal interlinks, and seven categories. Parsing its card-level related links, deduplicating repeated source/target pairs, and removing self-links yields **177 distinct directed peer relationships** from **54 distinct source routes**. These are different counting methods, not interchangeable totals. All targets map to a stable record in the [app index](app-index.md); two network routes have no observed related-link list. Broader directory entries are not given invented peer relationships.

## Editorial and technical rules

- Keep an obvious return path to the [documentation index](docs-index.md), [complete app index](app-index.md), [Discovery](discovery-benefits.md), and [Commercial](commercial-routing.md).
- Preserve observed directed edges separately from curated recommendations. Reciprocal links and implementation compatibility must not be inferred.
- Do not copy private data or attach live integrations because two records are linked.
- Retain held records with visible status rather than turning a failed route into an active CTA.
- Recheck anchors and all document paths after any rename. Record explicit source dates and the counting method.
- Use the [rollout gates](discovery-rollout.md) before any live integration or delivery claim, and the [promotional overview](promotional-overview.md) for bounded public language.
