# Draft organization and communication review

[Documentation index](docs-index.md)

This is a documentation-only recordkeeping guide. It does not deploy software, authorize outreach, change the intake schema, or select between separately proposed delivery-routing designs. Any routing or intake-policy reconciliation needs its own reviewed approval.

## One private tracker, three workflow phases

Keep contact-level records in an access-controlled executive tracker, not this public repository.

| Phase | Meaning | Release condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research / qualification | A candidate account and draft exist; no buying intent or qualified deal is implied | Validate fit, contact role, CRM account ownership, suppression, outreach basis, sender/footer and exact release approval |
| Internal review | An internal research or operational-status note | Confirm reviewers, current facts and exact intended recipients; retain internal-only content |
| Template / unassigned | Reusable text without a selected recipient or thread | Select and validate an eligible recipient and current context before personalizing |

Use research-priority fields independently of phase: A, B, partner research, internal and unassigned. Priority is a research queue position, not evidence of an active customer, contractual urgency or deal value. Keep topic as a separate field, such as inquiry triage, approved-document retrieval, RFQ/onboarding completeness, partnership qualification or operational continuity.

## Minimum private record

Track mailbox scope, stable draft/message/thread references, account, phase, research priority, topic, actual recipients, last observed UTC timestamp, CRM linkage and verified stage (or explicitly unverified), release holds, next action, accountable owner and source links. Unknown owners and dates remain unassigned; do not invent deadlines.

## Communication controls

- Read the current draft and actual To/Cc/Bcc headers before any release. Use the separately approved recipient and routing policy; do not silently add recipients or send duplicate copies.
- Recheck current replies, Sent history, account ownership, opt-outs, bounces and suppression across the authorized scope. A published corporate email does not establish consent, purchasing authority or deliverability.
- Research, a draft, provider acceptance, delivery and client agreement are distinct states. Record only the state supported by actual evidence.
- For complimentary discovery or a limited administrative AI trial, agree scope, duration, data permissions, success criteria and stop conditions in writing. Begin with approved sanitized or synthetic examples and human review; no automatic paid conversion or unapproved implementation is implied.
- Preserve relevant thread participants and applicable follow-up limits. Do not create follow-up clocks from unsent drafts or activate monitoring without approval.
- Never publish contact lists, mailbox identifiers, message/draft IDs, private tracker links, internal audit text, supplier costs, secrets or customer correspondence in this repository.

## Maintenance and continuity

Use the existing executive workspace and records before creating duplicates. Add compact current-state summaries while preserving dated historical evidence. Correct stale operational statements with a clearly dated addendum rather than treating a historical snapshot as current.

Save a factual checkpoint to the approved memory scopes only after confirming actual outcomes. Independently read it back, compare canonical content across stores, and record the real stopping point and pending approvals. A locally prepared checkpoint is not a persisted memory record.
